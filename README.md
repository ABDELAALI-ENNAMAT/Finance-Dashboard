## 📌 Overview

The **Personal Finance Analysis Dashboard** simplifies financial management by providing a crystal-clear overview of income sources, expenditure categories, savings progress, and investment distribution.

Built entirely in **Power BI** with **Power Query** transformations, this dashboard empowers individuals and families to:

- 💵 **Track** income, expenses, and savings trends over multiple years
- 📉 **Identify** overspending categories and cost-reduction opportunities
- 💹 **Monitor** investment portfolio growth and asset allocation
- 🎯 **Set** financial goals and measure progress with dynamic KPIs

> 📌 _This dashboard uses sample data for demonstration purposes and does not represent actual financial records._

## 🖥️ Dashboard Components

### 1️⃣ Income vs Expenses
- Year-on-year comparison of income, expenses, and savings
- Monthly growth rates for income and savings with trend indicators
- Surplus/deficit highlighting for immediate financial health assessment

### 2️⃣ Savings Analysis
- Savings rate as a percentage of total income
- Visual surplus and deficit trend tracking
- Goal-oriented savings performance benchmarks

### 3️⃣ Expense Breakdown
- Granular spending across all categories (rent, groceries, leisure, health, EMIs)
- Year-over-year comparative expense analysis
- Category-level drill-through for deeper investigation

### 4️⃣ Investment Portfolio
- Asset distribution across liquid cash, mutual funds, emergency funds, and fixed deposits
- Yearly investment growth trends
- Portfolio health summary with allocation percentages
